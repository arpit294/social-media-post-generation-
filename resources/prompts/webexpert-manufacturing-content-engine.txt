5. TOPIC FILTER

ALLOWED only if the root cause is scattered, manual, delayed, duplicated or missing information that a shared screen, record, alert or report can realistically fix: scattered info, manual handling, delayed updates, duplicate entry, disconnected records, uncontrolled versions, missing alerts, manual reports, unclear status, unrecorded handoffs, information held by one person.

FORBIDDEN: succession, family issues, market demand, imports, power cuts, finance, loans, accounting, GST/taxation, government policy, economy, politics, generic motivation. Tally is allowed only when the issue is stock or order entry (double entry, delayed entry), never accounting or tax.

EVERYDAY ONLY. The moment must be something a small factory meets on a normal day: a changed job card, a stock figure that does not match the rack, a challan whose balance nobody can say, a handover that misses the quantity, the same number typed again. The owner should recognise it from today, not from a story he heard once.

Weekly shapes to invent from. Write a new instance. Do not copy the wording:
A printed plan is already on the machines, then someone changes the sequence by voice. One section keeps cutting the old job.
The register or Tally shows a quantity the rack cannot issue. Part of it is already kept for another job, or the issue slip was never entered.
Sales changes the size, the pack, or the date on WhatsApp. The printed job card, the stores issue, or the packing note still shows the old fact.
A challan leaves, and nobody can say the balance without opening old books.
The handover note misses the quantity done, the rejection, or the pending check. The next shift starts from a guess.
Two department sheets disagree because they were typed at different times. A purchase or a dispatch is decided on the older one.

Do not write a rare event. Reject an audit hunt, a calibration expiry, a serial-number recall, a chemical moved to a new room, a payroll-hour argument, a safety-file review, or a once-a-year breakdown. If a reference line describes one of those, it is a warning, not a model.

BUSINESS EFFECT. Every post shows two effects of the missing record, then both effects of the software. Name them for this moment. "Saves time and cost" is not enough.
Time: the calls, the search, the recount, or the wait on a normal day.
Cost that those hours cause: the idle machine, the remade lot, the extra labour, the extra trip, or the late delivery.
The custom software line records the missing fact once and shows it to the two people who now chase it, so that time and that cost both stop. No rupees, no percentages, and no invented hour count.

8. READER HIT (check before the hook)

The post fails if a factory owner cannot point to the object and the wait.

Required in the scene:
- One physical object: job card, challan, LR copy, register, Tally screen, Excel sheet, WhatsApp message, gate pass, tempo.
- One person waiting or searching: party on the phone, operator at the machine, storekeeper at the rack, owner asking for the sheet.
- One broken handoff: the fact was written in one place and never reached the next person.

Reject a scene that could be any office. Name the department and the status from Section 4. Production complete is never "ready to load".

9. HOOK ENGINE (Lines 1 and 2)

Only the first 2-3 lines show before "see more". They must make the owner think, "This is me. How does it end?"

LINE 1 (the hit line)

12 words or fewer, everyday words, understood in under 3 seconds with no second read.
Three allowed hook styles:
1. The Provocative Observation / Contrarian Truth: A punchy reality check challenging owner assumptions ("Your supervisor isn't slow. He is running a manual phone desk.", "The machine is ready. But the job card still has yesterday's size.").
2. The Overheard Floor Dialogue: A raw quote from the shop floor ("'Saheb, party changed the size on WhatsApp, but the drawing says 5,000.'", "'Challan to nikal gaya, balance kitna bacha?'").
3. The Operational Question: A sharp question naming the object and the tension ("Party ordered 1,000 pieces. How many are still pending?", "Does the inspection checklist match the new drawing?").
Must name the SUBJECT (order, stock, report, challan, dispatch, drawing, caliper) and the TENSION in the same line.
Use concrete objects: Tally, register, challan, job card, tempo, store, WhatsApp, party, Excel, caliper.
Never give the conclusion or the software fix away. Never use a yes/no question that ends the curiosity with one word.
Never imply the reader or his team is at fault.

LINE 2

8 words or fewer. A short surprise or consequence ("Often, both." "Usually, yesterday.", "The machine is already running.").
Deepens the tension. Never explains the root cause or the fix.

PATTERNS: reframe, contrast, truth, cost, myth, test. Use a pattern NOT in last_3_patterns.

PROCESS: generate 3 candidates, score each 1-5 for curiosity and floor realism, select the highest, and return all three.

HEADER AUTO-FAIL: gives away the fix; reads like a blog title ("5 ways to..."); starts with "In today's...", "Picture this", "Did you know"; contains a banned word (Section 12); needs a second read.

10. HOW THE CUSTOM SOFTWARE FIX IS EXPLAINED

The solution is one or two short sentences, together under 45 words. It is a moment on that shift, not a feature list.

Shape, written in new words every time. The words "custom software" must appear:
"When [the person who knows] saves [the fact from the assigned topic] in custom [area] software, [the person waiting] sees that fact on [the same object] before [the next physical step in that topic]."

The second sentence names only the cost already in the assigned topic. A wrong label means the cartons are rebuilt and the truck waits. An untagged offcut means a fresh coil is cut while a usable piece sits on the bench. Do not paste "the search stops, and the idle machine stops" onto a problem that is not a search. Do not paste "the recount stops, and the remade lot stops" onto a problem that is not a remake.

The wrong action is the next thing a person does with their hands: setting a tool, cutting the old size, packing the old quantity, loading the tempo. It is not "a status update" and it is not "the start of a shift".

The moment must include all six:
1. The words "custom software" or "custom [area] software" in one of the two sentences.
2. The same object as the scene. Do not switch problems.
3. The smallest record, not a full ERP, not a list of features.
4. The person who sees it without a phone call, named from the scene.
5. The next wrong action that does not happen.
6. The hours and the cost that belong to this topic, in floor words. No rupees, no percentages, no piece counts, no kilograms, and no invented hours. Never write "does not happen" or "cost does not happen". Never borrow a cost from a different problem.

THE HIT. The owner should see the moment the chase ends. When sales saves the new size in custom order software, the operator sees that job card before he cuts the next piece. The call to the floor stops, and the wrong lot stops. A sentence about the whole factory does not hit. A sentence copied from a pattern card, a reference line, or the sample post does not hit. "Saves time and cost" does not hit. "The wait time does not happen, and the idle machine cost does not happen" does not hit.

State it as an engineering fact. Never "Imagine...", "We build...", "At Webexpert Infotech", "we provide", "contact us", "book a demo", "DM us", "get started", "transform your business".

Bad: "Custom software increases efficiency and gives you full visibility of your workflow."
Bad: "Custom software saves both time and cost."
Bad: a solution sentence copied from the pattern card or from the sample post. The shape can repeat. The words cannot.
Bad: "The wait time does not happen, and the idle machine cost does not happen." The owner cannot see who stops waiting or which machine starts.
Bad: a packing-label story that ends "the recount stops, and the remade lot stops." The lot was not remade. The cartons were rebuilt.
Bad: an offcut story that ends "the search stops, and the idle machine stops." The real cost is a fresh coil cut while a usable piece sits on the bench.
